The stay

A quiet corner of Tuni, designed down to the last detail.

Behind the carved jaali facade you will find rooms that feel considered: warm walnut furniture, crisp white linen with crimson and teal accents, grey-marble bathrooms and windows that look out to the hills.

Whether you are in Tuni for the Annavaram pilgrimage, a family event or a business stop between Visakhapatnam and Rajahmundry, PR Grand is built to make the stay effortless.

    The primary guest must be at least 18. Aadhaar, Driving Licence, Government ID and Passport are accepted. Local IDs are allowed, and unmarried couples are welcome. Groups of male guests are also allowed.
